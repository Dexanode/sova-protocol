# Participant guide

## Before the session

Confirm the interface says Whitechain Sepolia and audit deferred. Use only the
synthetic identifiers supplied by the operator. Never paste a seed phrase,
private key, keystore password, real personal data, salt, or real disclosure
payload into the dashboard or feedback.

## Tasks

1. Inspect the supplied attestation and identify issuer, schema, lifecycle
   status, expiry, and usability.
2. Evaluate the default consumer policy and explain why it accepted or rejected.
3. Change the policy so it rejects and identify the machine-readable reason.
4. If assigned, verify only the supplied synthetic disclosure fixture.

Record whether each task completed and approximate elapsed seconds. Ratings
measure interface clarity and confidence in understanding the displayed
decision; they do not rate a person's creditworthiness.

## Stop conditions

Stop immediately if the network or contract differs from the pilot scope, the
operator requests a secret or real identity data, registry state cannot be
read, or behavior may have caused a security/privacy incident. Mark
`incidentObserved=true` and notify the operator outside the feedback comments.
